About

SNDP eShakha

SNDP eShakha is a modern digital platform designed to empower SNDP branches with efficient member management, family surveys, and administrative tools. Built with the spirit of Sree Narayana Guru, it brings together technology and community.

Our mission is to provide a secure, mobile-friendly system that simplifies branch operations and fosters transparency.
